Common Questions

Questions contractors ask before they start.

i.How does a website audit work?
We run your website through a five-part diagnostic toolkit: technical foundation (Core Web Vitals, schema, mobile performance), AI search visibility (tested in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews), local SEO and Google Business Profile, conversion analysis, and competitive positioning against three local competitors. The result is a 12 to 20 page report with a prioritized action plan that you can take to your existing designer or to one of our vetted partner agencies.
ii.What's the difference between the free diagnostic and the $1,495 audit?
The free diagnostic is a one-page report with 5 high-level findings — what's broken on your site at a glance. The $1,495 deep audit is a 12 to 20 page report that explains why each issue matters, how it's hurting you, and exactly how to fix it, with every finding prioritized by effort and impact. Most contractors start with the free diagnostic and upgrade if they want the full picture.
iii.What happens after I get my audit?
You have two paths. You can take the audit findings to your existing website designer — they already know your business and the audit gives them a clear roadmap. Or, if you want specialist help, we'll introduce you to one of our vetted partner agencies. Either way, the audit is yours to keep and the choice is yours to make. We don't get involved in implementation.
iv.Why don't you build the sites yourselves?
By design. We don't sell rebuilds because we don't want a conflict of interest. Our income doesn't depend on convincing you that you need a $10,000 rebuild — so when the audit says you need one, you can trust the finding. And when it says you don't, you can trust that too. Independent auditors only work if they stay independent.
v.Can I use my existing website designer to make the changes?
Yes — and we recommend it when it makes sense. Most designers are perfectly capable of implementing audit findings once they have a clear roadmap. The audit is structured to be handed off directly. If your designer doesn't have specialist experience with schema markup, AI search optimization, or modern static-site architecture, that's when our partner agencies become useful.
vi.What kinds of contractors do you work with?
Contractors and home service businesses with 1 to 3 locations — general contractors, roofers, painters, plumbers, electricians, HVAC, hardwood flooring specialists, cleaning services, landscapers, and other trade specialists. We don't audit e-commerce sites, SaaS products, custom backend systems, sites with 50+ pages, or projects requiring HIPAA or PCI compliance.
